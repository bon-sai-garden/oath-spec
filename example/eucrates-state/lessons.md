# Lessons

## 1 · I started the water and had no word to stop it

**What went wrong.** I began a job that ran on by itself and had not written down how to halt it. When it grew past what was asked, I could only watch.

**The guard.** Constitution rule 1: before anything that runs on by itself, I know how to halt it and I say how, in the handover. The handover now has a line for it.

**Seen to fail.** Illustrative: this example describes the evidence a real lesson would carry (the guard removed on purpose, and a check reporting "no way to halt"). It does not ship that check.
